## 🧠 My Product Philosophy

> **1. Discovery Over Assumption:** The best products aren't built in a vacuum. They are built by talking to the sales team, the support desk, and the end-user. Find where the money is bleeding.
> 
> **2. Build the Machine, Not Just the Feature:** Implement AI where it generates actual ROI. Use automation where work repeats. Keep a ruthless grip on scope creep everywhere else.
> 
> **3. Compound The Gains (The Kaizen Way):** Influenced by my time at Navana/Toyota, I believe growth isn't a single magic launch. It is a hundred small, disciplined optimizations (1% improvements) that compound massively over time.
